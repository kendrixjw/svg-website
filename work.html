Family Reunion: Spades & Bones

Live

fam.sovereignvalorgroup.com ↗

A social multiplayer card and dominoes game on Next.js and TypeScript, with a complete Spades rules engine — jokers, nil and blind nil bidding, follow-suit and renege enforcement, full bag and sandbag scoring — plus Partnership All Fives dominoes. Seven AI opponents with distinct written personalities and over 300 rotating lines of table talk. Ten deck themes and ten table backgrounds rendered entirely in CSS, with keyboard navigation and screen-reader support throughout.
