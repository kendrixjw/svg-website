Privacy Policy

How Sovereign Valor Group LLC handles information collected through sovereignvalorgroup.com.

Last updated: August 20, 2026

Information We Collect

This website does not require an account and does not ask you to create one. We collect information in two situations:

Information you send us. If you contact us through the contact form or by email, we receive the information you choose to provide — typically your name, email address, organization, and a description of your project. The contact form opens a message in your own email client; it does not transmit your entry to a server on our behalf.

Technical information. Our hosting provider records standard server request data, such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested, as part of delivering the site.

How We Use Information

We use the information you send us to respond to your inquiry, evaluate whether we are a fit for your project, and communicate about work you have asked us to do. We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with third parties for advertising.

Third-Party Services

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads typefaces from Google Fonts. Those providers may process technical request data under their own privacy policies. We do not control their practices.

Cookies

This website does not set cookies for tracking or advertising purposes.

Data Retention

Correspondence you send us is retained for as long as needed to respond to and service your inquiry, and to meet ordinary business record-keeping needs.

Your Choices

You may request that we correct or delete correspondence you have sent us by emailing support@sovereignvalorgroup.com. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights over your personal information under applicable law.

Children

This website is directed to businesses and professionals and is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as our practices or services change. Material changes will be reflected in the "last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@sovereignvalorgroup.com.
