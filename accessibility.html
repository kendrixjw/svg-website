Accessibility Statement

Our commitment to keeping sovereignvalorgroup.com usable by everyone.

Last updated: August 20, 2026

Our Commitment

Sovereign Valor Group is committed to making this website usable by as many people as possible, including those who navigate with a keyboard, a screen reader, magnification, or other assistive technology.

Standard We Work Toward

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at Level AA where practical.

What We Have Implemented

Keyboard navigationEvery interactive element can be reached and operated by keyboard, with a visible focus indicator and a skip-to-content link.
Semantic structurePages use landmarks, a single main heading, and a logical heading order so assistive technology can navigate them.
Color contrastText and interface colors are chosen to meet AA contrast ratios against their backgrounds.
Reduced motionAnimation and scroll effects are disabled automatically when your system requests reduced motion.
Touch targetsButtons and navigation links are sized to be comfortably tappable on small screens.
Descriptive linksLinks and buttons state where they lead rather than relying on "click here" or "read more."

Known Limitations

Our venture products are separate applications with their own interfaces, and their accessibility may differ from this website. Third-party content we do not control may not meet the same standard.

Feedback

If you encounter a barrier on this site, we want to hear about it. Email support@sovereignvalorgroup.com with the page address and a description of the problem, and we will work to correct it.
